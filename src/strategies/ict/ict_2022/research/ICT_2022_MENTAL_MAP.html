ICT 2022 — Mental Map

A complete, standalone synthesis of the 2022 ICT Mentorship (68 decks) — readable without the codebase. It explains the whole model end to end, then maps each concept to the ict_2022 implementation. Per-deck notes live in DECK_NOTES.md; the read status + 12-rule shortlist in LEARNING_TRACKER.md. EpN / CS tags point at the source deck (Episode N / a Case-Study deck).

One sentence. Price is delivered by an algorithm that, at specific times of day, runs liquidity (stops) on one side of a level and then expands to the opposite pool — so an edge exists only where a price array (FVG / order block / old level) is reached at a key time, in the direction of the daily draw on liquidity. Everything below is the machinery for finding that one alignment.

1. The core thesis — time × price

"Algorithmic theory is based on TIME and PRICE. Price levels are useless until time is considered; time is useless unless price is at a key PD array. Blending the two yields astonishing precision." Ep39

ICT's central claim: there is no buying/selling pressure in the retail sense — price is delivered by an algorithm that seeks liquidity and rebalances inefficiencies. Ep39 The algorithm does not know how much volume sits above an old high or below an old low; it simply reaches for the pools (resting stop orders) and fills institutional interest at price arrays it left behind (FVGs, order blocks). Two consequences drive everything:

  • Liquidity is the magnet. Stops cluster above highs (buy-side, BSL) and below lows (sell-side, SSL), and at equal highs/lows (engineered liquidity). Price is drawn toward the obvious pool — the draw on liquidity. After repeatedly seeking one side, the algo eventually seeks the other. Ep39
  • Time gates the move. A price level only matters at certain macro times (the session opens / news embargo). The same FVG is tradeable at 10:00 and meaningless at 11:40. This is the reason a context-free pattern detector fails: it has the price half and none of the time half.

Power of 3 / AMD — the anatomy of every candle

Each timeframe's candle (daily especially) is built in three phases, fractally: CS Ep9

PhaseWhat happensWhere in the day (daily candle)
AccumulationConsolidation near the open; positions built quietly.Around the Midnight-NY open / pre-market.
Manipulation (Judas swing)A fake move that grabs liquidity on the wrong side (runs stops), trapping retail.The 08:30 / 09:30 push — the "Judas swing".

So a bullish day typically dips below the open first (manipulation = grab sell-stops in "discount"), then expands up to buy-side liquidity. The trader's job: identify the manipulation, then ride the distribution. The three session anchors that each start their own Power-of-3 are 00:00 (whole day), 08:30 (AM), 13:30 (PM). Ep11

2. The four nested layers

The model is a funnel. A trade needs all four to align; we historically built only #3.

  1. TIME — when the algo acts (anchors, sessions, macro times). §3
  2. NARRATIVE / BIAS — where price will reach for (the draw on liquidity; daily-rebalance bias; premium/discount). §4
  3. THE SETUP — the LTF trigger: sweep → MSS → displacement/FVG → entry. §5 we had this
  4. ARRAYS & TARGETS — the PD arrays you enter on and the draw you exit into. §6
The gap that explained our breakeven result. The old ict_2022 fired sweep→MSS→FVG anywhere in a killzone — no daily narrative, no anchor, no draw, no enforced premium/discount, no macro-time gate. Right trigger, random context. The redesign adds layers 1, 2, and 4 around the existing #3.

3. TIME — anchors, sessions, macro times

The anchors (the daily pivots)

  • Midnight NY Open (MNO, 00:00 ET) — the daily premium/discount pivot. Price above the MNO = premium (look to sell); below = discount (look to buy). The 24-hour cycle starts at midnight NY. Ep25
  • 08:30 ET — the news embargo and the AM manipulation anchor; the "last-ditch" Power-of-3 anchor. Ep19 At 08:30 on a bullish day, look left at the swing lows: the algo wants to absorb sell-stops there, then pair that with bias and trade into an FVG/OB. Ep19
  • 09:30 ET — the equities cash open; behaves like the 08:30 anchor for index/stock RTH. (For our RTH-only equity data, 09:30 is the session anchor; for 24/7 crypto/futures the true MNO/08:30 exist.)
  • 13:30 ET — the PM-session anchor (its own Power-of-3). Ep11
Anchor selection rule Ep17 Ep19: pick the anchor that is still "in play" vs price. If (bullish) the MNO is lower than where price trades around 08:30, the MNO is likely not a factor → use the 08:30 open. If on a bullish day price is still below the MNO after 08:30, you are in a heavy discount (a prime long location). Mirror for bearish. Code: anchor = MNO if still-in-play else 0830; price below both ⇒ heavy-discount long.

4. NARRATIVE / BIAS — the draw on liquidity

Bias is "where will price reach for", not "where will it close." Every-day perfect bias is unrealistic; think in terms of the weekly expansion target (a 3–5 day horizon). Without a confident HTF bias you are gambling — and "if you can't determine an HTF bias, you won't recognize a stop raid." Ep19 Ep39 Stand-aside is a first-class output, not a failure.

4a. Draw on liquidity — the targets

The draw is the obvious pool price is reaching for: PDH/PDL (prev-day high/low), PWH/PWL (prev-week), old highs/lows, and equal highs/lows (engineered liquidity — "the market does not like to leave relative equal highs; it runs through them later"). Ep20 Ep13 Prefer unmitigated draws: target a level where something was respected, not one whose stops were already taken (a prior SSL purge). Ep22

4b. Daily Rebalance Theory — the bias engine we were missing Ep25

The cleanest, most testable bias/draw algorithm in the entire mentorship. When a major high/low is taken on the weekly/daily, study the last 3 days:

  1. Is there a daily FVG (imbalance) in the last 3 days?
    • Yes → the draw is to rebalance that FVG (price returns to fill the gap). Direction = toward the gap.
    • No → use PDH/PDL and rebalance the previous day's down/up move.
  2. Purge & revert: after a major SSL purge (sweep of sell-side), revert to the high of the past 3 days (the purge day counts as day 1). Mirror for a BSL purge. Ep19 Ep25
  3. Validate the liquidity sequence: it matters which side is taken first. If bearish, you must see BSL taken first (the manipulation up) — otherwise the idea is invalidated and you may actually be bullish. Ep25
  4. Dealing-range EQ reaction: if the daily dealing range touches its 50% (equilibrium), expect the next day to react off it. Dealing range = the most recent, most energetic low↔high. Ep19

Simple corollary draws: "if the market takes out a daily low and creates a swing low, it likely wants the recent high" (and mirror). Ep37

4c. Premium / discount (location filter)

  • vs the anchor: above the MNO/08:30/09:30 open = premium (sell zone); below = discount (buy zone). Ep38
  • vs the dealing range: above 50% of swing-high→swing-low = premium; below = discount; 50% = equilibrium.
  • Short-term arrays Ep22: trading above an old high = a short-term premium array; below an old low = discount array. But price need not pull back — it can stay overbought and keep going. Premium/discount is a contextual gate that must agree with the narrative, not a mechanical reversal trigger.
  • Multi-stage delivery Ep35: when a leg delivers in two pushes, anchor premium/discount on the most recent sub-leg.
  • Rule: buys only in discount, sells only in premium; target the opposite (buy discount → premium draw).

4d. Inter-market / DXY

DXY (US Dollar Index) on the 1H = a risk-on/off and bias tool: when everything declines and DXY (safe haven) rises = risk-off; DXY is inversely correlated to indices. Use DXY for EUR/USD & GBP/USD bias too. Ep21 Ep20 (In our system this is approximated by the regime() VIX tag.)

5. THE SETUP — the entry sequence

The trigger, in order. Each step has a precise rule; the redesign keeps this engine and gates it with §3–§4.

1 Time & narrative are set

In a killzone, at/after a macro time; HTF bias + draw decided; price in the correct premium/discount location. No bias ⇒ no trade. Ep39

2 Liquidity sweep (the Judas / manipulation)

Price pierces a prior pool but closes back inside it — a stop-hunt (sell-side for buys, buy-side for sells), anchored to MNO / 08:30 / 09:30 / session open. A run through with a body close = continuation; a wick-take & reject = a sweep (reversal). Ep23 Ep33 A big run that leaves no tradeable FVG/pullback is "just sweeping" → stand aside. Ep36

3 Market-Structure Shift (MSS) — only after a sweep

Highest-value gating rule. "An MSS happens only when price takes out liquidity." Ep3 A structure break without a prior sweep is a mere MSB (continuation), not a shift. A true MSS = sweep + displacement: an energetic move that body-closes through the recent swing (a wick is not enough) and leaves an FVG. Ep3 Ep24

4 Displacement & the Fair Value Gap

  • Displacement = an "energy move away from the FVG and the high/low it took out." Ep20 No displacement ⇒ no pattern ⇒ no trade. Ep24
  • FVG geometry (strict 3-candle) Ep6: for a bullish FVG, candle-1's high, then a displacement candle-2, then candle-3 whose low > candle-1's high — the gap is candle-1-high → candle-3-low. Confirmed only on the 3rd-candle close. (Bearish mirrors.)
  • Quality filters: the FVG should sit above 50% of the displacement leg for buys (below for sells) Ep29; candle bodies (not just wicks) should respect it Ep22; an FVG that contains a swing low is higher quality (the swing is both trigger and stop). Ep9
  • Invalidation: only a body close through the gap kills it — a wick through is fine. Ep9

5 Entry — in the FVG / OTE, in discount/premium

  • Enter in the FVG and/or the OTE band (0.62–0.79 retrace of the displacement leg). CS
  • Must be in discount (buys) / premium (sells) of the dealing range and in the bias direction.
  • Stop: just beyond the sweep extreme; on a large imbalance, put the stop at the far edge of that imbalance, not the candle before. Ep18
  • Target: the opposite draw (§4a); partial at the first opposing array.
  • 09:30 "2nd-chance" model Ep15: if at the open an FVG has a swing low beneath it, that low gets swept (traps early buyers) → buy the original FVG once price reclaims it. If you miss the first entry, re-enter at the next BOS + fresh FVG as long as the R is still worth it. Ep29
  • Confirmation TF drill Ep18: bias on Daily → structure on 1H → 15m is the decision ("bellwether") chart → combine 15m + 5m FVGs; ICT also drills 4m/3m/2m/1m for precision and a final 1m MSS confirm.

6. PD ARRAYS & TARGETS

"PD arrays" = Premium/Discount arrays: the institutional reference levels price is delivered to. Order from the decks, high → low timeframe priority.

Order Block (OB)

  • Definition: "a change in the state of delivery" — the last down-close candle before an up-move (bullish OB), or last up-close before a down-move (bearish OB). Not every down candle is an OB. Ep13
  • 3-condition validity checklist Ep13 Ep18: (1) correct up/down-close candle; (2) it created an FVG / imbalance after it (the key test — no imbalance ⇒ not a valid OB); (3) aligned with daily bias.
  • Reference & invalidation: use the candle's open and its 50% = mean threshold (MT). A body close through the MT ⇒ the OB fails. Prefer bodies over wicks for the level. Ep35 Ep17
  • HTF OBs are real barriers ("high resistance = the OBs/orderflow that is holding") — don't target through them. Ep35

Fair Value Gap (FVG) / imbalance

See §5 step 4 for geometry, quality, and invalidation. An FVG is also a draw (Daily Rebalance Theory targets unfilled gaps) and, once violated, an Inverse FVG.

Inverse FVG (IFVG) & Breaker

  • IFVG: an FVG price has traded through (a later body close beyond the far side) flips polarity — a violated bullish FVG becomes bearish resistance, and vice-versa.
  • Breaker: when structure breaks, the broken swing level flips to S/R. FVG + breaker = "big confluence".

Old high / low (array flip + target)

Once price trades through an old high/low it flips the array (an old high becomes a discount array / support once reclaimed) and then acts as both S/R and a target — especially when paired with an FVG ("old high + FVG"). CS

Dealing range & equilibrium

Dealing range = the most recent energetic swing-high → swing-low; equilibrium = 50%. Premium above, discount below. Fib targeting: anchor LTL→ITH when bearish; −1.5 = a one-standard-deviation projection target. In low volatility draw the fib on bodies; in heavy news draw it on wicks. Ep12 Ep23

SMT (Smart Money Technique) — timed, biased confirmation

  • Compare correlated pairs — ES↔NQ, SPY↔QQQ, BTC↔ETH. Bullish SMT: primary makes a lower low (sweeps SSL) while the correlate makes a higher low (fails to confirm) = accumulation. Bearish mirrors with highs. Ep7
  • Not a standalone indicator — it's confirmation / the last confirmation, and only at the macro times (02:00, 08:30, 09:30, 10:00, 13:30) with a bias. Ep35
  • It also selects the symbol: when ES↔NQ diverge, trade the stronger (non-confirming) leg — "ICT longed ES because it was stronger." Ep29

7. The market-structure engine (precision technicians) Ep12

A three-tier swing hierarchy that defines what a "real" break is:

  • STH/STL (short-term), ITH/ITL (intermediate), LTH/LTL (long-term, daily-linked).
  • Between two STH sits an ITH; an ITL has a higher STL on each side. Every FVG rebalance promotes a swing to ITH/ITL.
  • LTH/LTL should not break against the trend; an ITH/ITL break = a significant MSS — if violated against you, stand aside.
  • Strength gauge: if an ITH is not higher than the prior two STH → the market is weak/bearish (mirror for bullish). In bearish conditions, every up-close candle should be respected; a violation is OK only if a STH sits above (a liquidity grab).
  • TF nesting: Daily FVG → 1H structure → 15m entries; cap the forecast at a ~5-day horizon.

9. News & events — catalyst, not filter

News is the alibi for the manipulation, a CATALYST — not something to hide from. ICT trades only high/medium-impact days; the morning Judas times off the 08:30 release. Our Phase-D data agreed: the setup is volatility-expansion and likes risk-off / news days
  • Leg sequence on news days Ep23: initial leg → another leg → fake-out leg → real setup. Don't take the first displacement; wait for the fake-out to complete (arm later on event days).
  • FOMC / NFP: FOMC AM is tradeable but be flat before the release. Ep15 NFP week (first of month): be done by Wednesday 08:30. Ep37 "Not an invitation to trade FOMC" — study, don't reflex-fade. Ep23
  • Fib anchoring by volatility: low vol → bodies; heavy news → wicks. Ep23
  • Ignore the two big news wicks when reading structure. CS
  • Causality (our hard rule): a release schedule is public ahead of time (forward-safe as a filter/regime flag); the actual result is look-ahead until the release timestamp.

10. Risk & trade management

  • Stop: beyond the sweep extreme / at the far edge of a large imbalance. Ep18 Exits a few ticks before the exact target level (front-run the pool). Ep17
  • Trail in stages Ep41 CS: at 50% of the target range → stop to 25%; at 75% → breakeven. But only go BE after price truly swing-mitigates your entry (not on first touch). Ep25
  • Pyramiding Ep26 CS: add with decreasing risk (1% → 0.5% → 0.25%); stack OBs within one leg.
  • Partials at the first opposing array; let a runner reach the full draw.
  • Confluence stack (the "golden" setup) Ep41: FVG + OB + OTE after a short-term MSS.
  • Mindset: "make trading boring"; don't trade sloppy PA; manage risk; don't touch capital when emotional. Ep19

11. Low-probability / stand-aside conditions CS

  • No confident HTF bias (gambling). Ep19
  • Counter-trend to the daily draw. (Lunch-break counter-trend is a specific exception, §8.)
  • Lunch 12:00–13:00.
  • A break without a prior sweep (MSB, not MSS); a big run with no FVG (just a sweep). Ep36
  • NFP week after Wed 08:30; London/AM after an FOMC whipsaw; sloppy/consolidation PA when expecting expansion.
  • "Setup not forming = a missed move, not a broken model." Do not loosen the displacement requirement to manufacture trades — that is the anti-overfit guardrail. Ep24

12. Two worked examples

Bullish day (buy)

  1. Daily Rebalance + draw point up (e.g. unfilled daily FVG above / PDH as draw); bias = long.
  2. Price opens, dips below the MNO/09:30 anchor → heavy discount location.
  3. At/after a macro time (08:30→10:00), it sweeps a swing low / SSL (absorbs sell-stops) and closes back up.
  4. A 5m/15m MSS: an energetic up-candle body-closes over the recent swing high, leaving a bullish FVG in the upper half of the leg.
  5. Price retraces into the FVG/OTE (0.62–0.79), still in discount; enter long. Stop below the sweep low; target the buy-side draw (PDH / old high / equal highs). SMT (SPY↔QQQ) confirms at the macro time.

Bearish day (sell)

  1. Daily Rebalance + draw point down (unfilled daily FVG below / PDL); bias = short. Liquidity sequence: BSL taken first confirms bearish.
  2. Price opens, rallies above the anchor → premium location (the Judas up).
  3. At a macro time it sweeps a swing high / BSL and closes back down.
  4. A 5m/15m MSS down: body-close under the recent swing low, leaving a bearish FVG in the lower half of the leg.
  5. Retrace into the FVG/OTE in premium; enter short. Stop above the sweep high; target the sell-side draw. SMT confirms.

13. The 12 codeable rules (the redesign spec)

  1. TIME × PRICE gate. A setup fires only when a PD array is reached at a macro time (00:00/08:30/09:30/10:00/13:30). The old model had no time gate — the core fix.
  2. Bias is a prerequisite; stand-aside is first-class. No confident HTF bias ⇒ no trade. Don't loosen displacement to force trades (anti-overfit).
  3. Daily Rebalance Theory bias/draw (§4b). Last-3-days FVG ⇒ rebalance it; else PDH/PDL; purge-&-revert; validate liquidity sequence.
  4. Anchor + heavy-discount read (§3). Pick MNO vs 08:30/09:30 by what's in play; below both = heavy-discount long; needs an LTF MSS to trigger.
  5. MSS requires a prior sweep + displacement that leaves an FVG. Run-with-FVG = continuation; sweep+reject = reversal; big run no-FVG = stand aside.
  6. FVG quality is quantifiable. Above/below 50% of the displacement leg; bodies respect it; contains a swing; body-close-through invalidates.
  7. OB validity = it created an imbalance after it; ref = open + 50% MT. MT body-close-through ⇒ fail.
  8. Premium/discount is a contextual gate, not a reversal trigger. Target unmitigated draws, not already-swept levels.
  9. News/regime = catalyst. Initial→another→fake-out→real leg sequence (arm later); pre-FOMC-speak = narrow range; NFP week done by Wed 08:30; fib on wicks in heavy news.
  10. Day-type regime switch. Outside day ⇒ consolidation ⇒ fade EQ edges + 15:00 MOC; bullish-day template (AM rally→lunch→PM SSL sweep→rally).
  11. SMT = timed, biased confirmation that selects the symbol. Only at macro times, with a bias; trade the stronger leg.
  12. Confluence (Tier 3) & risk. "FVG+OB+OTE after a short-term MSS is golden." Trail 50%→25%, 75%→BE (BE only after swing-mitigation).

The redesign — Tier 1 → 3 (all toggles; theory-led "full" defaults; validate in Phase E)

The trigger engine (§5) is unchanged; we wrap it in the missing layers, each a toggle so Phase E can ablate every one on the reserved 2025/26 holdout. Defaults encode the full ICT setup (not a tuned subset) to avoid curve-fitting.

Tier 1 — narrative gates _time, _bias

  1. Macro-time gate — entries only at 08:30/09:30/10:00/13:30 windows (the time half of the thesis).
  2. Session anchor + premium/discount — buys in discount of the anchor (below 09:30/MNO open), sells in premium; heavy-discount read.
  3. Daily Rebalance bias + draw — direction from last-3-days FVG / PDH-PDL; trade only with the draw; target the draw.
  4. News as catalyst — prefer high-impact days; arm later in the news leg sequence.

Tier 2 — the narrative engine _narrative

  1. MNO/anchor Power-of-3 day engine — detect the morning Judas vs the anchor as the qualifying sweep.
  2. Daily-narrative wrapper — take the sweep→MSS→FVG trigger only when bias + draw + premium/discount + macro-time all agree.
  3. Day-type regime — consolidation (fade EQ) vs expansion (model); AM/PM session structure; no-trade lunch; max-trades/day.

Tier 3 — confluence & array upgrades _smt, _pd_arrays

  1. Timed, biased SMT — ES↔NQ / SPY↔QQQ at macro times only; symbol selection.
  2. OB mean-threshold invalidation; FVG above 50%-of-displacement filter; FVG+OB+OTE golden confluence; old high/low as target; 15m bellwether confirm.
  3. Management — partial-at-first-array, staged trail (50→25, 75→BE), front-run target; pyramiding (later).

Implementation lands in: _time.py (anchors/macro-times/PD-vs-anchor), _bias.py (Daily Rebalance draw), and an expanded _strategy.py composing the toggles; _smt.py / _pd_arrays.py get the Tier-3 upgrades. Each layer is measured independently before the one definitive multi-ticker 4–5yr Phase-E run.
