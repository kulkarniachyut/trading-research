ICT 2022 — Mental Map

Engineering synthesis from the 2022 ICT Mentorship decks · for the ict_2022 strategy · built to be implemented & backtested, not traded discretionarily. See DECK_NOTES.md for per-deck notes and LEARNING_TRACKER.md for read/pending status.

Core thesis. Price is delivered by an algorithm around TIME and LIQUIDITY. The daily candle is a Power-of-3: Accumulation (consolidation near the open) → Manipulation (a "Judas swing" grabs liquidity on one side) → Distribution (expansion to the draw on liquidity) → Close. Smart money buys discount / sells premium; retail does the opposite. News is the alibi for the manipulation.
The gap that explains our breakeven result. ICT is a daily-narrative, session-anchored, draw-on-liquidity machine. Our current ict_2022 is a context-free pattern detector — it fires sweep→MSS→FVG anywhere in a killzone, with no daily narrative, no Midnight-NY-Open anchor, no draw, no premium/discount enforcement. We built the LTF trigger and skipped the narrative that is supposed to gate it. Right trigger, random context.

The four nested layers (we built only #3)

  1. TIME — anchors.
    • Midnight NY Open (MNO) = the daily pivot. Premium above, discount below.
    • 08:30 ET data release = the morning manipulation/Judas. 09:30 equities open behaves the same.
    • Sessions: London (02–05), NY AM (08:30–noon), London Close (10:00–11:00 = reversal of the daily range; 10–12 on news days), NY PM (13:30–16:00).
    • No-trade 12:00–13:00 (lunch). Afternoon usually follows the trend; algo "starts" ~19:30 prior day.
    • Max ~4 trades/day (2 AM, 2 PM). Judas/Power-of-3 is fractal across sessions, daily/weekly/monthly candles.
  2. NARRATIVE / BIAS — top-down ("where will price REACH for").
    • Weekly draw on liquidity = the obvious pool price is reaching for: PWH/PWL, old highs/lows, equal highs/lows. "Every-day bias is unrealistic" — think weekly expansion target, not close.
    • DXY for risk-on/off (DXY↑ = risk-off safe haven; inversely correlated to ES/indices).
    • Daily-bias trigger: a sweep of a daily high + 3-bar reversal (confirmed when the 4th candle trades lower) → expect lower; vice-versa sweeping a low.
    • Trade WITH the draw / daily trend. Counter-trend is explicitly a low-probability condition.
  3. THE SETUP — the 5-step entry (this is what we have).
    1. Time of day (in a killzone).
    2. Narrative set (HTF bias / draw aligned) — we under-implement this.
    3. Liquidity swept = the Judas/manipulation grabs a pool (sellside for buys, buyside for sells), anchored to MNO / 08:30 / session open.
    4. Displacement / MSS (energetic move that leaves an FVG; market-structure shift).
    5. Entry in the FVG, in discount (buys) / premium (sells) of the dealing range, with 1m MSS confirmation; drill timeframe to use the highest-TF FVG present.
  4. ARRAYS & TARGETS — the draw.
    • PD arrays: FVG, Order Block (+ mean threshold = 50%-body; a body-close through it invalidates), Breaker (FVG+breaker = "big confluence"), old high/low (flips premium↔discount array once traded through; then acts as S/R and as a target — esp. with an FVG), Inverse FVG.
    • Dealing range = swing-high → swing-low; equilibrium = 50%.
    • Target = the opposite array / draw: buy discount → target premium liquidity/FVG/OB; sell premium → target discount. Old highs / PWH / equal highs. Partial at the first opposing array.
    • SMT = confirmation on a correlated pair (ES↔NQ; SPY↔QQQ): compare swing lows when bullish, swing highs when bearish. "Not a standalone indicator." Strong at news time → take the stronger (non-confirming) pair.

News & low-probability filters

  • News = CATALYST (not a filter). ICT trades only high/medium-impact days; the morning manipulation times off the 08:30 release ("news is an alibi for manipulation"). Ignore the 2 big news wicks. our Phase-D data already confirms: news/high-VIX days were the strategy's best
  • Low probability — stand down: NFP week (Wed NY / Thu / Fri); the London & AM session after an FOMC whipsaw; counter-trend; lunch 12–13.

Glossary (fast reference)

  • MNO = Midnight New York Open (00:00 ET) — daily premium/discount pivot.
  • Judas swing / protraction = the fake move before the real move; the manipulation that grabs liquidity.
  • Power of 3 / AMD = Accumulation → Manipulation → Distribution (the daily candle anatomy).
  • Draw on liquidity = the pool price is reaching for (the target/bias).
  • Displacement = an energetic, obvious move that leaves a Fair Value Gap (imbalance).
  • MSS = Market Structure Shift (break of the most recent swing in the new direction).
  • OTE = Optimal Trade Entry (62–79% retrace of the displacement leg).
  • SSL / BSL = Sell-Side / Buy-Side Liquidity (stops below lows / above highs).
  • SMT = Smart Money Technique — divergence between correlated pairs (confirmation).
  • Mean threshold = 50% of an order-block body (invalidation level).
  • Killzone = a session window where the algo seeks liquidity (London / NY AM / London Close / NY PM).
